# Trust Policy (Internal)

## Trust Score Ranges
- LOW: trustScore < 10
- MEDIUM: 10 ≤ trustScore < 50
- HIGH: trustScore ≥ 50

## Principles
- Trust scores are internal only
- Exact values are never shown to users
- Thresholds may change based on data
- Trust effects are silent and non-negotiable

## Usage
- Deposits are adjusted by trust tier
- Booking access is gated by trust tier
- Trust decays over time (see Day 11)
