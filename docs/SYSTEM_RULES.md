# RENT IT — SYSTEM RULES (CANONICAL)

## Platform Authority
- Rent It is the final authority in all disputes.
- All transactions, deposits, and payouts are controlled by Rent It.

## Communication
- Direct communication between renters and owners is strictly prohibited.
- All coordination occurs via the platform only.

## Fit Policy
- Fit-related issues are categorically non-disputable.
- Fit compatibility indicators are advisory only.

## Condition Lock
- Item condition is locked before each rental.
- Only platform-captured photos are valid evidence.
- User-uploaded condition evidence is invalid.

## Deposits
- Deposits are mandatory for all rentals.
- Deposit amounts may increase or decrease based on trust behavior.

## Trust System
- Trust is earned progressively and decays over time.
- Trust data is internal, platform-bound, and non-transferable.
- Leaving Rent It permanently forfeits accumulated trust.

## Money Handling
- All rental fees and deposits are held in platform escrow.
- Owners receive payouts only after successful return verification.
